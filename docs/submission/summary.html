01

Project title

Pantha Sahayam (పంట సహాయం): voice-first crop advice for farmers in Telugu, Hindi and English.

02

The problem we are solving

When pests or diseases attack, small farmers must decide what to spray, how much, and when it is safe to harvest. Usually they guess, or ask the pesticide shop that sells the spray. Wrong products, overdoses and spraying too close to harvest waste money, harm health and leave residues on food.

Existing farm apps are text-heavy, English-first and need a login, while many farmers are more comfortable speaking their own language than typing. Generic AI chatbots can also invent pesticide doses, which is dangerous.

Our answer: the farmer speaks the problem and hears back the officially registered spray, the dose per litre and the waiting period before harvest. Every number comes from the Government of India's CIB&RC pesticide registry (2,243 registered uses) and is checked automatically.
03

Who will use it

  • Small and marginal farmers in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and the Hindi-speaking states, using any phone browser with no app install and no login.
  • Family members helping them, who can switch the answer's language at any time.
  • Agriculture extension workers and Krishi Vigyan Kendra staff answering farmers' questions in the field.
  • Agri-input dealers who want to recommend the correct registered product and dose.
04

The Gnani AI models we use

Gnani Prisma v2.5Speech-to-text
Turns the farmer's spoken Telugu, Hindi or English into text. /stt/v3 API, clips up to 30 seconds.
Gnani Timbre v3.0Text-to-speech · voice "Lakshmi"
Speaks every answer, follow-up question and choice prompt in the farmer's language, including when the language is switched.
Gnani EvonLanguage model · evaluated
An optional, guarded hook can make answers more conversational. We deliberately kept Evon out of the dose-giving path, so no AI model can change a pesticide number.
Design principle: AI listens and speaks; the advice itself always comes from the checked government registry.
